# EDA - 2026-04 (training month only)

Generated by `python scripts/eda.py`; every number below is computed from the data in that run
(raw values in `reports/eda_stats.json`, cleaning counts in `reports/cleaning_log.md`).
The held-out test month was not opened.

## Size and missing values
- 3,831,240 raw trips. Columns with missing values: {'passenger_count': 799786}.
- Missingness is one block of rows: passenger_count is missing for 20.9% of trips, and
  rate code, store-and-forward flag and the two surcharge fields are missing on exactly the same rows (True). We keep these trips and let the
  model see passenger_count as NaN. A further 11,696 rows have passenger_count 0 or above 6 and are also set to NaN.

## Outliers in the target (duration = dropoff - pickup)
- Non-positive durations: 49,511. Shorter than 60 s: 88,176. Zero-distance trips: 93,515.
- Longer than 3 h: 1,251; longer than 24 h: 21 (forgotten meters / multi-day records).
- 12 rows have a pickup timestamp outside 2026-04 (the file contains stray dates such as the year 2001).
- Among trips that survive the duration filter, 696 have an implied speed above 80 mph, so some
  noise remains. We do not filter on distance or speed because that would use post-trip information to define the evaluation set.
- Thresholds chosen: keep 60 s <= duration <= 3 h. After cleaning 3,741,803 trips remain.

## Target shape
- Cleaned duration quantiles (minutes): 1% 2.4, 10% 5.4, 25% 8.7, median 14.3, 75% 22.6, 90% 34.5, 99% 75.5, 99.9% 118.2.
  Mean 18.1 min, std 14.6 min: strongly right-skewed, so RMSE will be
  dominated by the long tail while MAE reflects typical trips. Plot: `reports/duration_distribution.png`.

## Time patterns (`reports/time_patterns.png`)
- Weekday median duration is longest at 11:00 (16.33 min) and shortest at 2:00 (11.78 min).
  On weekends the longest median is at 16:00 (15.28 min). The weekday and weekend profiles differ,
  so hour x weekend interactions matter.
- Median duration by weekday (Mon=0): {0: 14.13, 1: 14.43, 2: 14.6, 3: 14.95, 4: 14.27, 5: 14.0, 6: 13.13}.

## Geography
- 40,101 distinct (pickup zone, dropoff zone) routes; 45.1% of them have fewer than 5 trips in the full month,
  so route-level medians are sparse in the tail and need a fallback.
- Airport trips (pickup or dropoff in JFK, LGA or EWR) are 8.0% of trips with a median of 35.5 min
  vs 13.4 min for the rest.
- Same-zone trips are 3.7% of trips (median 4.8 min).

## Consequences for modelling
- Clean at 60 s / 3 h and report everything on the cleaned scale; the cleaned evaluation set is therefore a *filtered* population (see README limits).
- Heavy right tail: compare a log-target model with a direct MAE-objective model.
- Sparse routes: baseline (b) needs a fallback chain; tree models get zone geography as a smoother.
- April 2026 contains no US federal holiday, so an `is_holiday` feature is constant in training and cannot be learned from this month.
